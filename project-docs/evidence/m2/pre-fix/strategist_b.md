*   **Pain Points:** "Atmospheric Overstimulation" in large chain cafes and "Isolation Fatigue" in home offices. They are looking for "Micro-Communities"—smaller, curated spaces and brands that feel exclusive and quiet rather than high-traffic.

*   **Fleet Coffee:** Positions as the "Technical Expert." Content is highly scientific, focusing on extraction ratios and bean chemistry. **Gap:** Their Instagram lacks a "human" or "lifestyle" element, making it less approachable for the casual remote worker who just wants a better desk-side companion.

*   **Coffee ASMR & "Study With Me" Loops:** High engagement with short-form video (Reels) featuring the sounds of Austin—rain in the Hill Country or the hum of a local shop—overlaid with coffee brewing.

*   **Niche Over Mass:** Growth among Austin remote workers will come from "Niche-Checking." Content must feel like it’s for "those who know," using hyper-local Austin references (e.g., "The construction on I-35 is loud, but our roast is smooth").
*   **The "Workday Utility" Angle:** Since the target audience uses Instagram for discovery, Ember & Oak should position their beans as a "productivity tool" for the home office, not just a beverage.
*   **Visual Transparency:** In 2026, "unfiltered" and "raw" roastery footage (the mess, the heat, the real work) outperforms highly polished studio photography among Gen Z and Millennial remote workers who value "The Real Austin."
